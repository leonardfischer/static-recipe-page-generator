## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Plain flour | 200 g |
| Baking powder | 2 tsp |
| Sugar | 1 tbsp |
| Salt | 1 pinch |
| Buttermilk | 300 ml |
| Eggs | 2 |
| Melted butter | 30 g |

## Instructions

Each item of this ordered list becomes one `HowToStep`.

1. Whisk flour, baking powder, sugar and salt in a large bowl.
2. Beat buttermilk, eggs and melted butter in a second bowl.
3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ones and stir *just* until combined – a few lumps are fine.
4. Let the batter rest for 15 minutes.
5. Heat a lightly greased pan over medium heat and fry about 3 tbsp of batter per pancake until bubbles appear, then flip.

## Glossary

Buttermilk
: The slightly sour liquid left over from churning butter. It makes the pancakes tender.

Resting
: Gives the baking powder time to start working and the flour time to hydrate.

## Tips {.text-success}

- Keep finished pancakes warm in the oven at 80 °C.
- ~~Use cold milk~~ Room-temperature ingredients mix more evenly.
